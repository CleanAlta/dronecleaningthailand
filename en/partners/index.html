Partner with CleanAlta in Thailand

We bring a method proven on French sites, cleaning protocols per material, drone pilot training, and references such as RATP and Saint-Gobain. We are looking for a Thai company that brings clients, local presence and the ambition to build a new service line together.

Start a conversation WhatsApp the founder

Who we would like to meet

Facility management and cleaning companies

You already clean buildings in Thailand and want a method for high, glazed or fragile surfaces that your teams cannot reach safely.

Drone operators

You hold Thai registrations and fly professionally, and you want a cleaning activity with recurring revenue instead of one-off missions.

Property and hospitality groups

You own or manage many buildings and want to test a new cleaning method across your portfolio.

What CleanAlta brings

  • A working method: tethered drone, water-fed pole and aerial platform, with heated osmosed water
  • Protocols per material: limestone, stoneware, concrete, lacquered steel, glass
  • Training: TELEPILOTE, the training centre also founded by Vincent Goleau, has trained professional drone pilots in France since 2014
  • Equipment know-how: drone, ground unit, water treatment and spares selection
  • References: RATP, Saint-Gobain, CNRS, CHU de Toulouse, Veolia, Bouygues
  • Commercial material: case studies, video, method statements

What we look for

  • A Thai company with an existing client base in buildings, hospitality or industry
  • People on the ground who can be trained as pilots and ground operators
  • Knowledge of local permits, insurance and procurement
  • The will to invest time and money in a new service, not just resell it

The form of the partnership is open: licence, joint venture, or operating partnership. We will propose the structure after a first meeting, depending on what each side brings.

For investors

CleanAlta SAS was founded in December 2024 in France by Vincent Goleau, a professional drone pilot for more than ten years. It covers France and Switzerland and works for large public and private clients such as RATP and Saint-Gobain.

The Thai launch is in its opportunity phase: we are looking for pilot sites and a local partner. We are open to discussing an investment in the Thai activity with people who know the market. We do not publish projections on this site; we share figures in a direct conversation.

If you speak French or German, there are short versions of this page in French and German.

Partner questions

Do I need drone experience to become a partner?

No. We train pilots and ground operators. What matters is access to clients and a team that can work carefully on building sites.

Is this a franchise?

Not at this stage. For Thailand we want one strong partner and a model we build together. A wider network may come later.

How do we start?

A video call with the founder, then a visit of two or three potential pilot sites together. If both sides want to go on, we write a simple agreement for the pilot phase.
