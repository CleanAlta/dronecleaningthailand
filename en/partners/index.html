Strategic partnership and investment in Thailand

CleanAlta built its drone façade-cleaning method on demanding French sites: a railway station at night, a research centre, a working hospital. We are selecting one strategic partner and a small number of investors to launch the activity in Thailand.

Request the investor brief Contact form

Who we want to work with

Organisations that already have buildings, clients or capital in Thailand, and want a new service line with recurring revenue.

Property and hospitality groups

Owners and managers of hotels, office towers and condominiums who want to apply one cleaning method across a portfolio.

Facility management companies

FM and cleaning groups that need a safe method for high, glazed or fragile surfaces their teams cannot reach.

Industrial groups and estates

Operators of factories, warehouses and rooftop solar in the EEC and other industrial zones.

Investors

Corporate investors and family offices with knowledge of the Thai property, hospitality or industrial market.

What CleanAlta brings

  • An operating method: tethered drone, water-fed pole and aerial platform, with heated osmosed water
  • Protocols per material: limestone, stoneware, concrete, lacquered steel, glass
  • Pilot training through TELEPILOTE, a French drone training centre operating since 2014
  • Equipment know-how: drone, ground unit, water treatment and spares
  • References: RATP, AP-HP, Saint-Gobain, CNRS, CHU de Toulouse, CNSMDP (Paris Conservatoire), Veolia, Bouygues
  • Sales material: case studies, site video, method statements

What we look for

  • An established client base in buildings, hospitality or industry in Thailand
  • Staff on the ground who can be trained as pilots and ground operators
  • Knowledge of local permits, insurance and procurement
  • A commitment to invest in a new service line, not only to resell it

Investment approach

CleanAlta SAS was founded in France in December 2024. It operates in France and Switzerland for public and private clients, including RATP and Saint-Gobain. The Thai activity is at market-entry stage: pilot sites and the local partner are being selected.

Three formats are open for discussion: strategic investment in the Thai activity, a joint venture with a Thai group, or a licence. The structure is set after a first meeting, depending on what each party brings.

We do not publish projections on this website. The investor brief, the launch roadmap and the figures are shared in a direct meeting, under a non-disclosure agreement.

Process

  1. Introductory call with CleanAlta management to check the fit on both sides.
  2. Investor brief under NDA: method, references, launch roadmap and figures.
  3. Joint visit of two or three candidate pilot buildings in Thailand.
  4. Agreement for the pilot phase, then the long-term structure.

Questions from partners and investors

Does a partner need drone experience?

No. CleanAlta trains pilots and ground operators. What matters is access to clients and a team able to work carefully on building sites.

Is this a franchise?

Not at this stage. For Thailand we want one strong partner and a model built together. A wider network may follow.

Is financial information available?

Yes, in a direct meeting and under NDA. Nothing financial is published on this website.
