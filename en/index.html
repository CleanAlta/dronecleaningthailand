Why Thai buildings need a different method

Heat, humidity and the monsoon are hard on façades. Algae grows on shaded walls, dust and traffic soot settle on glass, and buildings near the sea collect salt. Cleaning more often is the answer, but rope access teams and building gondolas are slow to set up and put people at height.

Thailand also has a lot of buildings to look after. The Department of Tourism counted 17,126 hotels and 711,729 rooms in the first half of 2024, before counting condominiums, office towers, hospitals, malls and factories. Many of these buildings have glass atriums, curved façades or rooftop solar that standard methods struggle with.

A tethered drone does not replace every method. It adds one: fast on large flat or curved surfaces, no anchors on the roof, nobody hanging in front of guest rooms. We combine it with poles and platforms where they work better.

Source: Department of Tourism, H1 2024, quoted in LH Bank, Industry Outlook 2025, five-star hotel business.

Questions we hear first

Do you already operate in Thailand?

Not yet. CleanAlta operates in France and Switzerland. We are preparing the Thai launch: selecting pilot sites, finding a local partner, and registering drones and pilots with the Thai authorities before any flight.

Is drone cleaning legal in Thailand?

Yes, within a framework. Drones must be registered with the NBTC and the CAAT, the operator needs third-party insurance of at least 1,000,000 THB, and urban flights or flights near people need specific permission. We handle all of this before a pilot job. Details on our compliance page.

Which buildings are a good fit?

Buildings from about four floors up with large glass or cladding surfaces, curved façades, atriums, closed courtyards, and roofs or solar arrays that are hard to reach. Hotels, condominiums, office towers, hospitals, universities and factories are typical.

Do you use chemicals?

On glass, no: we use heated osmosed water at low pressure, which dries without streaks. On roofs, we apply slow-acting biocide or enzymatic treatments against moss and algae, chosen for the surface and used according to the product label.

How much does it cost?

It depends on surface area, height, access and dirt level, so we quote after a site survey. Pilot sites get a tailored offer in exchange for allowing us to document the job.
