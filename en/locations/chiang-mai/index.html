Chiang Mai · Pilot sites wanted

Drone façade and glass cleaning in Chiang Mai

Chiang Mai has a dust and smoke season from February to April that coats every window in the city, then a rainy season that turns the film into streaks. Hotels, universities, hospitals and the new condominium towers along the Ping river all clean glass more often than they would like.

The right buildings in Chiang Mai

Chiang Mai is lower-rise than Bangkok, which is good news for drone work: most buildings are between four and fifteen floors, few have gondolas, and many have courtyards, canopies and glass roofs. These are the surfaces where a tethered drone replaces days of scaffolding.

  • Hotels and boutique resorts in Nimman, the old city and along the river, cleaned before breakfast service
  • University and hospital campuses with large glass buildings that stay open all year
  • Condominium towers with glass balconies and painted walls
  • Malls and showrooms with glass roofs and canopies

After the smoke season, a single pass with heated purified water removes the grey film without detergent. We recommend a second pass after the rains.

Airspace in Chiang Mai

Chiang Mai International Airport sits south-west of the old city, closer to the centre than any other major Thai airport. The 9 km rule therefore covers the old city, Nimman, the university area and most of the urban core. Every job in the city will need CAAT permission with that in mind, and lead times will be longer than elsewhere. Sites further out, towards Mae Rim, San Kamphaeng or Hang Dong, are easier.

We will say in the remote assessment whether a given address is realistic, before anyone travels.

We have not flown in Chiang Mai yet. Registrations, insurance and permissions will be in place before the first job. Details on the compliance page.

Chiang Mai questions

Is it realistic to fly inside the old city?

Only with CAAT permission because of the airport. We will not promise a date before the permission is in hand.

When is the best time of year?

Right after the smoke season, in April and May, and again after the rains in November. Early mornings in the cool season are ideal for flying.

Can you do a university campus in one visit?

Large campuses are done building by building over several days, with a schedule agreed with the facility team. That is how we work on research centres in France.
