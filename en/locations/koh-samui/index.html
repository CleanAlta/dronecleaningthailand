Koh Samui · Pilot sites wanted

Drone cleaning for hillside villas and resorts on Koh Samui

Samui's best properties sit on steep hills above Chaweng, Lamai, Bophut and Taling Ngam, with sea views, glass walls and roofs that no aerial platform can reach. Cleaning them means ladders, ropes and risk. A tethered drone arrives in a van and works from the terrace.

Why hillside properties are a drone job

On a hillside, access is the whole problem. Roads are narrow, driveways steep, and a 40 m platform cannot get near the building. Scaffolding has to be carried by hand. So roofs go green, glass goes grey, and cleaning happens less often than it should.

A tethered drone needs a few square metres of flat ground, a water point and a power supply or our generator. From a terrace or a pool deck it reaches the roof, the glass walls and the rendered façades of a villa in a morning, then moves to the next one.

  • Tiled and shingle roofs: slow-acting algae and moss treatment, nobody on the roof
  • Floor-to-ceiling glass and sea-view windows, purified water, no streaks
  • Rendered and painted walls with algae on the shaded side
  • Resort pavilions, spa roofs, covered walkways and pool buildings

Samui airspace and weather

Samui Airport is on the north-east of the island, between Bophut and Chaweng. The 9 km rule covers a large part of the north-east coast, including Chaweng, Bophut and Choeng Mon, so properties there need CAAT permission and more lead time. The west and south of the island are outside that circle, with the standard permissions still required.

Rain peaks in October and November on Samui. The best window for large jobs runs from January to August, with early-morning slots to avoid the afternoon breeze.

We have not flown on Samui yet. Registrations, insurance and permissions will be in place before the first job. Details on the compliance page.

Samui questions

Can one visit cover several villas?

Yes, that is the point. An estate or a management company with ten or more villas is the ideal pilot: one mobilisation, many roofs.

What do you need from the villa?

A few square metres of flat ground, a garden tap, a socket or space for our generator, and someone to keep guests and staff out of the zone below the drone for a few hours.

Do you treat moss or just rinse it?

We treat it. A slow-acting product keeps working for weeks and the rain clears the dead growth. Rinsing alone brings it back within months in Samui's climate.
