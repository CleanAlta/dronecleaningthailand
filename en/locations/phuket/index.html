Phuket · Pilot sites wanted

Drone cleaning for resorts, villas and condominiums in Phuket

Sea air, humidity, heavy rain from May to October and strong sun the rest of the year: Phuket buildings fight salt, algae and dust all year. Resorts clean constantly, and most of them still do it with ladders, ropes and scaffolding on properties full of guests.

What the drone changes for a resort

A resort is a collection of difficult surfaces: glass lobbies and restaurants, villa roofs in tile or thatch-look material, pool buildings, spa pavilions, covered walkways, and often a hillside layout where nothing with wheels can go. Cleaning it means weeks of people on ladders and ropes in front of guests.

With a tethered drone, the team arrives in a van, sets up a perimeter around one building at a time, and cleans glass and roofs from the ground. Guests see a drone for a few hours, not scaffolding for a week. Early-morning slots fit before breakfast service.

  • Glass façades, lobbies, restaurants and sea-view windows, rinsed with purified water, no streaks
  • Roofs of villas and pavilions: algae and moss treatment, nobody walking on tiles
  • Solar canopies and roof arrays on back-of-house buildings
  • Rendered and painted walls stained by algae on the shaded side

Phuket airspace and sites

Phuket International Airport sits in the north of the island, near Mai Khao and Nai Yang. Thai rules require permission to fly within 9 km of an airport, so resorts on the north-west beaches need that permission and more lead time. Patong, Kata, Karon, Rawai, Cape Panwa and Phuket Town are outside that circle but still need the standard permissions for flights near people and buildings.

Wind matters more than in Bangkok. We plan sea-facing façades in the morning and stop when the breeze picks up. The dry season from November to April is the natural window for large jobs.

We have not flown in Phuket yet. Registrations, insurance and permissions will be in place before the first job. Details on the compliance page.

Phuket questions

Can you clean a resort while it is full?

Yes. We work building by building, early in the morning, with a closed perimeter. In France we clean hospitals and stations while they operate.

Does salt need a special treatment?

Salt dissolves in water. Heated purified water removes it without detergent. The issue is frequency: sea-facing glass needs more passes per year than city glass.

What about thatch or shingle roofs?

We assess them case by case. Low pressure and a slow-acting treatment work on most, but we test a small area first and we will say no if the material cannot take water.
