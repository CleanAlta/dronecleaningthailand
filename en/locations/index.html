Where we work in Thailand

Honest version: nowhere yet. We are selecting the first pilot sites, and the first regions we serve will be the ones where those sites are. The five areas below are where the buildings, the climate and the demand make drone cleaning most useful, and where we are actively looking.

Capital

Bangkok

Glass towers, condominiums, hospitals, malls. The densest building stock in the country and the most complex airspace, with two airports.

How we will choose the first regions

Three things decide where we start: a client ready to be a pilot site, a local partner with a team to train, and airspace where permits are realistic. A hotel in Phuket with a clear beach-side take-off area is easier to authorise than a tower next to an airport.

Thai rules keep drones 9 km from airports without permission, 50 m from people not involved in the operation for drones between 2 and 25 kg, and require specific permission for urban flights. Each city page below says what that means in practice. Details and sources are on the compliance page.

Thailand's hotel stock alone is large: 17,126 hotels and 711,729 rooms in the first half of 2024 according to the Department of Tourism. Add condominiums, offices, hospitals, universities, malls and factories, and the question is not whether there is work, but where to do the first jobs well.

Source: Department of Tourism, H1 2024, quoted in LH Bank, Industry Outlook 2025.
