About CleanAlta

CleanAlta is a French building cleaning company that works at height without putting people at height. We use tethered drones, water-fed poles and aerial platforms, and we pick the tool for the façade, not the other way round.

The story

Vincent Goleau has flown drones professionally for more than ten years. In 2014 he founded TELEPILOTE, a training centre for professional drone pilots in France. CleanAlta started from a simple observation: cleaning glass and façades at height is slow, expensive and dangerous for the people doing it, and drones could change that.

In December 2024 he founded CleanAlta SAS to answer that problem with a method, not a gadget. The first jobs were for demanding clients: RATP, Saint-Gobain Recherche, CNRS, CHU de Toulouse. Each one taught us something and became a protocol.

Our signature in France is “L'humain d'abord”: people first. The drone is a means. What matters is that nobody gets hurt, the building is clean, and the site keeps running.

The founder

Vincent Goleau has flown drones professionally for more than ten years, among the first in France. He founded TELEPILOTE in 2014, a training centre whose instructors have accumulated more than 40,000 flight hours, and CleanAlta SAS in December 2024. He still flies on CleanAlta jobs and reads every request that comes through this site.

How we work

1

The right tool

Drone, pole or platform, chosen façade by façade. We say no to the drone when it is not the best option.

2

Written protocols

Each material has its own protocol: water temperature, distance, passes, products. We test before we clean.

3

Rules first

Permissions, insurance and a method statement before any flight, in France as in Thailand.

Why Thailand

Thailand has what makes drone cleaning useful: tall glazed buildings, a climate that dirties façades fast, a large hotel sector, and a growing number of rooftop solar plants on factories. It also has clear drone rules that are enforced, which protects serious operators.

We are in an opportunity phase: we are looking for pilot sites and a Thai partner, and we will grow from there. No promises of nationwide coverage, no invented case studies.

About us, briefly

Is CleanAlta a subsidiary of TELEPILOTE?

No. CleanAlta SAS is an independent company. TELEPILOTE SAS is a minority shareholder with 10% and acts as UAS operator for CleanAlta's flights in France.

Where are you based?

Our head office is in Maule, west of Paris. We work across France and in Switzerland, and we are preparing Thailand with a local partner.
