RATPParis Saint-LazareOctober 2025Night work

RATP: 400 m² of glazing in one night

The Paris transport operator needed glazing above its tracks cleaned without stopping trains. With its usual method, the same work would have taken several nights.

CleanAlta cleaned 400 m² of glass in a single night, with no interruption to traffic. RATP reports that intervention time was divided by five.

Photo: glazing cleaned by drone on another Paris site.

Saint-Gobain RechercheAubervilliersDrone + poleRecurring contract

Saint-Gobain Recherche: from a test to a contract

Saint-Gobain's research centre near Paris has large glazed façades over several floors. We combined a tethered drone for the upper levels and a water-fed pole for the lower ones and the entrance.

Heated osmosed water, no detergent. After the first job, Saint-Gobain Recherche signed a contract for two cleaning passes a year.

AP-HPHôpital européen Georges-PompidouParis 15e2026

AP-HP: a working hospital, cleaned from its own helipad

Hôpital européen Georges-Pompidou, part of Assistance Publique – Hôpitaux de Paris, has glazed stair towers and façades over several floors, with patients and staff inside at all times.

Flying a 24 kg drone over a Paris hospital requires the approval of the Paris Police Prefecture. CleanAlta obtained it, and the drone took off from the hospital helipad, tethered to its ground unit. Nobody worked at height during spraying. The contract covers 8,570 m² of glazing.

The national music conservatoire

The Conservatoire national supérieur de musique et de danse de Paris sits in the Cité de la musique district at La Villette. CleanAlta cleaned its façade by tethered drone, with the pilot and crew on the ground. The 98-second film below was shot on this job.

Hotels, a factory and a hangar

CleanAlta has also cleaned hotel buildings, a factory and a hangar in France. These clients are not named on this website; details are available in a direct meeting.

CHU de ToulouseRangueil hospital

Hospital windows on the 8th floor

Windows on the eighth floor of a working hospital, cleaned without closing services and with no one working at height during application. For hospitals, this removes the risk of a worker on a rope outside patient rooms and the noise of a gondola.

CNRSISMO, Orsay

The “periodic table” façade

The Institute of Molecular Sciences at Orsay has a façade of coloured glass panels laid out like a periodic table. We cleaned it with a drone and an aerial platform working together, and restored the transparency of every panel.
