Safety and compliance: Thai drone rules, and how we apply them

Cleaning a building by drone means flying a heavy aircraft close to a façade, often in a city. In France we do it under EU rules with prefecture authorisations. In Thailand we will do it under CAAT and NBTC rules. This page sums up what the law requires and what we do on top.

What Thai rules require

The summary below is based on the Thai government's public guidance on drone registration and flight rules, dated 27 June 2025. Rules change and temporary restrictions apply in some areas, so we check current CAAT notices before every job.

TopicRequirement
RegistrationDrone registered with the NBTC (radio equipment), then drone and operator registered with the CAAT through the UAS Portal. Registration is valid for two years.
InsuranceThird-party liability insurance of at least 1,000,000 THB.
VisibilityDaylight flights only, with the drone in visual line of sight.
HeightSpecial approval needed above 90 metres.
AirportsNo flight within 9 km of an airport without authorisation.
Distance from peopleFor drones of 2 to 25 kg, keep 50 metres from people not involved in the operation.
Urban areas and gatheringsFlights over cities, crowds and gatherings require permission. Special operations are handled by the CAAT UAS unit.

Sources: Thai government portal thailand.go.th (27 June 2025); CAAT UAS unit. Temporary restrictions, such as those announced near some border zones in November 2025, show that local rules can change at short notice.

What this means for a cleaning job

A façade cleaning drone is in the 2 to 25 kg class and works close to buildings, usually in a city. In practice, every job needs registered equipment and pilots, insurance, and a specific permission for the site. The 50-metre rule means we close and control the ground area below the work zone, and we plan work at times when the area is quiet.

A tethered drone helps: it is held by its cable and hose, so it cannot drift away, and its power comes from the ground. That does not replace any permission, but it makes the safety case clearer for the authorities and for building owners.

Our status today

CleanAlta has not yet registered drones or pilots in Thailand. Registrations, insurance and permissions will be completed with our Thai partner before the first pilot job. We will not fly before that.

How we work in France

In France, CleanAlta's flights are carried out under the EU “specific” category, including the STS-01 standard scenario, with prefecture authorisations for urban sites. TELEPILOTE SAS, the drone training centre founded in 2014, acts as UAS operator for these flights. We have obtained authorisations for sensitive urban sites in Paris, including hospitals.

Our safety rules on every site

  • Written method statement shared with the site before the job
  • Closed and marked perimeter below the work zone, with a ground operator in charge
  • Test flight and test area before cleaning
  • Wind and rain limits: we stop when conditions are outside the drone's limits
  • Pre-flight checklist on drone, tether, hose and ground unit
  • Coordination with the building's security and facility teams

Water and products

On glass we use only purified water. On roofs, we use slow-acting biocide or enzymatic products, chosen for the surface and applied according to the label, with protection of pools, water tanks and planted areas. We bring our own water treatment unit.

Compliance questions

Who is responsible if something goes wrong?

The registered operator, who must hold third-party liability insurance. For each job we provide the insurance certificate and the method statement before work starts.

Can you fly near a hospital or an airport?

Only with the right permission. Near airports, the 9 km rule applies. In France we have flown on hospital sites with prefecture authorisation; in Thailand the same kind of approval will be requested from the CAAT.

Do you need to close the street?

Not always. We need a controlled area under the drone. Depending on the building, that can be a courtyard, a car park section or a pavement closed for a few hours, agreed with the site.

Have a building in Thailand that is hard to clean?

Send us a few photos, the address and the approximate height. We will tell you honestly whether a drone, a water-fed pole or a platform is the right tool, and whether your site could join the pilot program.
