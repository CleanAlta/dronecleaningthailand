Building cleaning services: the right tool for each surface

CleanAlta is not a drone company that cleans. We are a cleaning company that uses drones where they make sense, and water-fed poles or aerial platforms where they do a better job. Two service lines, one method: purified water, low pressure, and the operator on the ground.

Service 1

Façade and glass cleaning

Curtain walls, windows, glass roofs, atriums, aluminium and composite cladding, painted or stone façades. Heated osmosed water at low pressure, no detergent on glass, and a rinse that dries without streaks.

Roof and solar panel cleaning

Water and products

On glass and most façades we clean with heated osmosed water. Osmosis removes the minerals that leave white marks when tap water dries, so there is nothing to wipe. Heat helps lift grease and pollution, so we do not need detergent. Our nozzles work at low pressure and the water goes onto the surface, not into the air.

On roofs, moss and algae come back unless the roots are treated. We spray slow-acting biocide or enzymatic treatments that keep working for weeks after the job, and we choose them for the roof material and the surroundings. Products are always used according to their label.

What we do not do

We focus on cleaning. The Thai offer does not include drone inspection, thermography or 3D modelling. If you need those, we can point you to specialised operators.

How a job runs

  1. Photos and plans

    You send photos, the address, the height and the surfaces. We say quickly whether drone cleaning makes sense.

  2. Site survey

    We check take-off areas, obstacles, wind exposure, water and power supply, and who uses the ground below.

  3. Method statement and permits

    A written plan: tools per façade, safety perimeter, timing, flight permissions and insurance.

  4. Cleaning

    Ground team of two or three: a pilot, a ground operator who manages the hose and the perimeter, and a pole or platform operator when needed.

  5. Report

    Before and after photos, surfaces cleaned, observations on the condition of the façade.

Service questions

Can you clean while the building is occupied?

Yes. We work with a closed perimeter on the ground and we agree the schedule with the site. In France we clean hospitals, research centres and stations while they stay open, sometimes at night.

How often should a façade be cleaned in Thailand?

It depends on exposure, the surface and how the building is used. Hotels and offices often want glass cleaned several times a year. We recommend a schedule after the first job, once we have seen how fast the building gets dirty.

What do you need on site?

A take-off area on the ground or a roof, access to a water point, a power supply or space for our generator, and a site contact who can close access below the work zone.
