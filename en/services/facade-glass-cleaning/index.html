Façade and glass cleaning by drone

Heated osmosed water, low pressure, no detergent on glass. A tethered drone does the upper floors at 500 to 800 m² per hour, a water-fed pole and a platform finish what the drone cannot reach. Nobody hangs in front of your windows.

What we clean

  • Glass curtain walls on office towers, hotels and condominiums
  • Windows on upper floors, including where gondolas cannot run
  • Glass roofs, atriums and canopies, often impossible to reach from inside
  • Cladding: aluminium, composite panels, painted or coated metal
  • Mineral façades: render, concrete, stone, with a protocol per material
  • Closed courtyards and curved façades where a platform cannot drive in

In France we have written cleaning protocols per support: limestone, flamed stoneware, concrete, lacquered steel. The same discipline applies in Thailand: we test a small area first and adjust temperature, distance and passes before cleaning the whole façade.

The method, step by step

The drone is tethered: power and water come up through a cable and hose from the ground unit. It can stay in the air as long as the job requires, and it cannot fly away.

1

Purified water

Tap water goes through reverse osmosis to remove minerals. Without minerals, water dries without spots, so there is no need to squeegee or wipe.

2

Heat, low pressure

Heated water lifts grease, soot and pollution film. Low pressure protects seals, joints and coatings. The economical nozzle puts the water on the surface, not in the air.

3

Rinse and finish

Top-down passes with overlap, then a final rinse. The pole or the platform handles frames, corners and lower levels in the same visit.

Façade cleaning questions

How fast is drone façade cleaning?

Between 500 and 800 m² per hour on suitable surfaces, depending on how dirty the façade is, the method and the protocol. Set-up, perimeter and finishing by pole come on top.

Will it leave streaks on the windows?

Not with osmosed water. Streaks come from minerals in tap water and from detergent residues. We use neither on glass.

Can the drone damage the façade?

The drone does not touch the building. It sprays from a short distance at low pressure. We test a small area first on sensitive materials and adjust the protocol.

What about wind and monsoon rain?

We do not fly in strong wind or rain. In Thailand we plan jobs in the dry season where possible and keep flexible dates during the monsoon.
