Boilerplate

CleanAlta is a French building-cleaning company that uses tethered drones, water-fed poles and aerial platforms to clean façades, glass, roofs and solar panels without putting anyone at height. Founded in December 2024 by professional drone pilot Vincent Goleau, it works for clients such as RATP, AP-HP, Saint-Gobain, CNRS, CHU de Toulouse and the Paris Conservatoire. CleanAlta is preparing its launch in Thailand with a strategic partner and investors.
