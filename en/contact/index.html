Contact

Request a site survey

Tell us about your building. We read every request ourselves and answer with an honest first opinion: is a drone the right tool, and could your site join the Thailand pilot program?

Faster by message

WhatsApp is the quickest way to reach us, and you can send photos directly.

WhatsApp +33 6 86 50 55 71 eco@cleanalta.net

What to send

  • Address or Google Maps link
  • Type of building and number of floors
  • Surfaces to clean: glass, cladding, roof, solar
  • 3 to 5 photos of the façades
  • When you would like the work done

We are based in France (UTC+1, UTC+2 in summer). Thailand is 5 to 6 hours ahead. The Thai afternoon is our morning.

What happens after you write

No automatic sales sequence. A person reads your request and answers it.

  1. First answer

    We look at your photos and the location, check the airspace around the building, and tell you plainly whether a drone, a pole or a platform fits, or whether none of them does.

  2. Short call

    If the project makes sense, a 20-minute call to understand the building, the surfaces, the constraints of the site and your timing.

  3. Pilot or quote

    For sites in Thailand, we propose a place in the pilot program with its conditions. Outside Thailand, we send a quote from CleanAlta France.

  4. Site visit

    Before any commitment on price or date, we visit the site to check take-off areas, water, power and safety.

Before you write

Do you have an office in Thailand?

Not yet. We are setting up with a local partner. Site visits in Thailand are organised once a project is qualified.

Can I write in Thai?

Yes. Write in Thai or English. We answer in English for now, and in Thai once our local partner is in place.

What happens to my data?

We use your details only to answer your request. The form is hosted by Google Forms. See our privacy notice.
